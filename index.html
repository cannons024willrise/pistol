Storage Details

What gets saved:
• Pattern memory (all learned relationships, confidence values, catalyst history)
• Training sessions (name, description, date, variations)
• Relationship history (everything you've built in the mesh)

How it works:
Every time you add a relationship or complete a training session, the data is automatically written to IndexedDB. When you reload the page, everything is restored instantly.

Browser storage limit:
Most browsers allow 50MB+ per origin. For typical use (thousands of patterns and hundreds of sessions), you'll use just a few MB.
