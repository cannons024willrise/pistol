Pistol

Feed PistolAny text, in any shape: articles, books, chats, logs, notes, code, lists, subtitles, JSON, HTML, any language. Nothing is cleaned up or cut out. Pistol guesses each next word before it sees it, learns from the miss, and keeps the order of everything, so a longer text simply teaches it more. Reading runs at tens of sentences a second and slows as its memory grows. What it learns is kept in this browser, and Export (in Memory) saves a copy.
